# Emoji data

`emoji.json` is generated by `node tools/build-emoji.mjs <emoji-test.txt>` from
Unicode's `emoji-test.txt` (https://unicode.org/Public/emoji/latest/). Unicode
data files are © Unicode, Inc. and used under the Unicode License
(https://www.unicode.org/license.txt). Skin tone and hair variants are left out
to keep the panel quick; the emoji themselves render with your installed emoji
font (for example Noto Color Emoji).
